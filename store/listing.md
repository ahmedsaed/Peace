## Full description (4000)

```
Peace is an expense tracker that keeps its mouth shut. Every record you make lives on your phone — there is no account to create, no server holding your spending, no ads and no analytics.

Peace of mind. Piece of cake.

LOG A SPEND IN SECONDS
• A calculator keypad: type 120 + 35 and save the answer.
• One key walks you from the amount to the account, the category and saved.
• Pick a calculator or phone layout, and the side the operators sit on.
• Works in split screen next to your bank's notification.

SEE WHERE IT WENT
• Budgets per category, showing what is left this month.
• Spending ranked by category, with shares that add up to exactly 100%.
• Search by category, account, date, amount or note, with a total of what it found.
• Carry your balance from month to month, so you always know what you hold.

EVERY ACCOUNT, ADDING UP
• Cash, bank accounts and credit cards. A card reads as what you owe, with the credit still available.
• Transfers between your own accounts are counted once, never as spending.
• Refunds net against the purchase they reverse.
• Multiple currencies, with the rate kept on each record.
• Repeating bills and income come due as suggestions to approve — never posted behind your back.
• Tags, receipt photos and a daily reminder if you want one.

LET AI DO THE TYPING (OPTIONAL)
With your own Google Gemini API key — free to create — Peace can:
• Read a photographed receipt and fill in the amount.
• Turn bank notifications into records for you to approve.
• Answer questions about your spending with Penny, the assistant, using figures from your own ledger.
Only Google sees what you send, and only once you add a key. Everything else works without one.

YOURS TO KEEP
• Back up to your own Google Drive automatically, encrypted with a passphrase if you like.
• Or save one file holding everything, receipts included, wherever you choose.
• Export to CSV for any spreadsheet.
• Hide every amount with one tap when someone is looking over your shoulder.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Nothing leaves your phone until you turn something on: reminders, AI, bank notifications and backups each start switched off. Peace contains no advertising or tracking code at all.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Peace is free software under the GPL-3.0. Read the code at github.com/ahmedsaed/Peace.

Made by Merope — peace.merope.dev
```
